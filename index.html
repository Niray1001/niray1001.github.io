Projects & Experience

DC-DC Converter Project – The Hive

Breadboard prototype of a DC-DC converter developed at The Hive.

At The Hive, I participated in a hands-on engineering project focused on building a DC-DC converter. The project introduced me to power electronics and gave me an opportunity to explore how electronic circuits convert electrical power between different voltage levels. I personally assembled the converter on a breadboard, working directly with electronic components to build a functional circuit.

Through this process, I developed a better understanding of how individual components work together within a circuit and how their connections affect its overall operation. Building the converter allowed me to move beyond theoretical concepts and gain practical experience with circuit assembly and prototyping. It also helped me appreciate the importance of understanding component functions and circuit behavior when developing electronic systems.

One of my biggest takeaways was the value of hands-on experimentation in engineering. Working directly with the circuit helped me connect electrical concepts to a practical application and strengthened my interest in power electronics, circuit design, and hardware development. This experience has motivated me to continue exploring electronics through more advanced projects and opportunities at Georgia Tech.

RoboJackets – PCB Development

KiCad schematic featuring the RP2040 microcontroller and supporting circuitry.

As a member of the RoboJackets electrical subteam, I have been working with a group of students to design a printed circuit board (PCB) using KiCad. Our work involves developing a circuit design around an RP2040 microcontroller and incorporating the supporting electronic components needed for the board. Through this experience, I have been introduced to the process of translating circuit concepts into a PCB design.

Working collaboratively with other members of the electrical subteam has helped me develop both technical and teamwork skills. I have gained experience navigating KiCad, working with electronic components, understanding circuit connections, and contributing to the development of a shared hardware project. I have also had opportunities to explore soldering and hardware prototyping, which have helped me better understand the relationship between circuit design and physical electronics.

One of the most valuable aspects of this experience has been seeing how a group can work together to develop an engineering solution. It has allowed me to learn from other students, strengthen my understanding of electronics, and gain exposure to a more structured engineering design process. I look forward to continuing my work with RoboJackets and contributing to more advanced hardware projects.

Tools & Skills: KiCad, PCB Design, Circuit Design, Soldering, Prototyping
